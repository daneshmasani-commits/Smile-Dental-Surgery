About the practice

A husband-and-wife practice built on long-standing patient relationships

Smile Dental Surgery is a small, family practice in the heart of East Ham — led by husband-and-wife team Russ and Zak Sullman. We've been caring for patients here for over two decades, and many of our patients have been with us almost that long.

We offer both NHS and private treatment. Whether you need a routine check-up, a hygienist appointment, or more specialised care, we take time to listen, explain things clearly, and work at a pace that suits you.

Our practice is small by design — that means you'll be seen by people who know your name and your history. For nervous patients especially, that familiarity makes all the difference.
